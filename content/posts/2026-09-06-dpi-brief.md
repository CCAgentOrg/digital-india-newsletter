# DPI Brief — September 06, 2026

**The past 24 hours saw movement across six of the seven DPI layers**: UPI set a fresh all-time volume record for August, UIDAI's paperless verification framework found its first education-board adopter, DigiLocker became compulsory infrastructure for a national exam, a listed PSU disclosed a probable breach flagged by CERT-In, and the health and agri-DPI stacks logged fresh milestones.

## 1. UPI Logs Record 24.51 Billion Transactions in August (L2: Payments)

NPCI's monthly data shows [Unified Payments Interface processing a record 24.51 billion transactions in August 2026](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/economy/upi-sets-new-record-with-24-51-billion-transactions-in-august-11985650) — the first time volumes crossed the 24-billion mark, up 3.6% from July's 23.66 billion and 22% year-on-year. Value stood at [₹29.8 lakh crore](https://www.rediff.com/business/report/upi-transactions-near-record-value-all-time-high-in-august/20260901.htm), a whisker below the all-time high of ₹29.9 lakh crore set in May and July. A decade after launch, the ecosystem now spans 741 live banks.

Two distribution stories stand out. [PhonePe's UPI 123PAY integration on Lava feature phones](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/lava-feature-phone-users-can-now-make-upi-payments-through-phonepe-heres-how-it-works/articleshow/133680030.cms) brings internet-free payments to basic handsets — the A7 Torch (2026) ships first, with the A2 Smart following by September-end. On the international front, [Uzbekistan went live on August 30 as UPI's eleventh market](https://www.ibtimes.co.in/upi-gambit-how-indias-payment-revolution-goes-global-what-comes-next-905362), joining Bhutan, Nepal, Singapore, UAE, France, Sri Lanka, Mauritius, Qatar, Cambodia and Greece — though cross-border volumes (14.86 lakh in FY26, nearly double FY25) remain under 0.01% of total transactions. Global ambition is real; domestic scale is still the story.

## 2. Punjab School Education Board First to Adopt UIDAI's Offline Verification Framework (L1: Identity)

The [Punjab School Education Board has become India's first education board to onboard UIDAI's Offline Verification Seeking Entity (OVSE) framework](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Sep/04/pseb-becomes-first-education-board-in-india-to-adopt-uidais-paperless-identity-verification-framework), enabling consent-based, paperless identity verification for admissions and examinations. Under the [OVSE framework](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/09/05/the-offline-verification-seeking-entity-ovse-framework), authorised entities verify Aadhaar Verifiable Credentials that the holder shares via the Aadhaar App — fully or selectively, with explicit consent — and crucially, verification happens locally without querying UIDAI's central identities database. The [Aadhaar App has crossed 5 crore downloads](https://www.facebook.com/IBEFIndia/posts/1395498329433878). For consumers, this attacks the photocopy economy: fewer physical Aadhaar copies circulating means fewer documents to leak, and selective sharing means a school exam board no longer needs your full demographics.

## 3. DigiLocker Becomes a Mandatory Verification Rail (L3: Documents)

DigiLocker graduated from convenience to compulsion on two national fronts this week. IIT Madras has made [DigiLocker-based verification mandatory for all Indian candidates registering for GATE 2027](https://education.sakshi.com/en/gate/education-news/gate-2027-registration-big-change-introduced-digilocker-verification-now-mandatory-all-candidates-199104) — applicant details are fetched directly from DigiLocker, with the registration window open until September 27. Separately, India Post's recruitment for [23,757 Gramin Dak Sevak posts](https://indianexpress.com/article/education/india-post-gds-recruitment-2026-registrations-begin-indiapost-gov-in-applications-released-september-2-10856697) will verify educational documents through DigiLocker or API Setu. The state exam-and-jobs complex routing identity through DigiLocker is a quiet but consequential default: when lakhs of candidates must maintain verified document lockers, DigiLocker's accuracy and grievance-redressal become consumer-protection issues, not just features.

## 4. ABDM Nears 94 Crore ABHA IDs; AgriStack Incentives Flow (L5: Sectoral)

Reviewing the mission's progress, Health Minister J.P. Nadda said ABDM has emerged as [one of the world's largest digital health ecosystems with over 93.95 crore ABHA IDs](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/ayushman-bharat-digital-mission-emerges-as-one-of-worlds-largest-digital-health-ecosystems-with-over-93-95-crore-abha-ids-j-p-nadda), 105 crore linked health records, 5.33 lakh registered facilities and 9.85 lakh professionals. The Mission Steering Group flagged the familiar sticking points: private-sector participation and state-level implementation — visible in [Haryana, where 97.9% of targeted government facilities are onboarded while private adoption lags](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/low-private-sector-adoption-could-slow-digital-health-mission-says-acs/articleshow/133745357.cms). In agriculture, the Centre sanctioned [Telangana a ₹175 crore AgriStack incentive](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/telangana/centre-sanctions-one-more-incentive-of-175-cr-to-tg-for-agristack-implementation/article71411542.ece) for completing its AgriStack-based fertiliser distribution milestone, taking the state's total to ₹599 crore, and deployed a [real-time fertiliser tracking tool linked with iFMS and AgriStack](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/fertiliser-ministry-deploys-analytical-tool-to-prevent-fertiliser-shortage-126083100003_1.html) to detect shortages and curb leakages.

## 5. GeM at Ten: ₹4 Lakh Crore FY26 GMV, GeM 2.0 Takes Shape (L4: Commerce)

The Government e-Marketplace, marking its tenth year, has [crossed ₹4 lakh crore in GMV within the first ten months of FY26](https://www.facebook.com/govGeM/posts/1510722204427377), against cumulative procurement of over ₹15 lakh crore; Uttar Pradesh alone has crossed ₹1 lakh crore in GeM procurement. GeM officials are now socialising "GeM 2.0" — [new bidding features discussed with large PSU buyers like DVC on September 2](https://www.facebook.com/DamodarVC/posts/1348048844077077). The export story continues too: Indonesia's ION, built on the ONDC blueprint with diplomatic backing from the July Modi-Prabowo pacts, has [incorporated as a sovereign digital commerce DPI](https://investortrust.id/business/115235/open-network-incorporates-as-digital-public-infrastructure-to-upend-e-commerce-with-google-backing?page=english-edition) — early validation that India's open-network template is becoming exportable public infrastructure.

## 6. Trust Layer: PSU Breach Disclosure After CERT-In Alert (L7: Trust)

Gujarat Mineral Development Corporation informed stock exchanges on September 4 of a [probable data breach following a CERT-In alert on its website](https://businessupturn.com/business/gujarat-mineral-development-corporation-reports-possible-data-breach), with core operations unaffected. The disclosure is noteworthy as an artefact of the new compliance clocks — CERT-In's six-hour incident reporting window and the DPDP Rules' 72-hour intimation to the Data Protection Board — now visibly shaping listed-PSU behaviour. With the [BRICS Summit in Delhi on September 12–13](https://www.news18.com/india/16-lakh-attempts-in-a-minute-after-g20-cyber-onslaught-india-builds-bigger-digital-shield-for-brics-ws-bl-10307410.html), agencies are building a larger digital shield; the G20 benchmark — 16 lakh attack attempts per minute on the summit site, contained by I4C, CERT-In and NIC — sets the threat bar.
